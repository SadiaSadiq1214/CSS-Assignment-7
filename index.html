Cake Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/ 4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/ 2 cup butter
  • 1/ 2 cup milk

Preparation Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix butter and sugar until smooth.
  3. Add eggs and milk, then mix well.
  4. Add flour and baking powder, and stir until combined.
  5. Pour the batter into a cake pan.
  6. Bake for 30–35 minutes.
  7. Let the cake cool and serve.
